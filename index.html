How it works

Decide at the table, then watch it reach the school and the home

The board decides

In 2026: what to do about AI tutors. In 2027: what happens to the teachers. The second question is worded for the first answer.

The impact plays

The camera pushes into the board's screen: the school, then a family at home with the fee bill, the report card and what the parents say.

A hundred futures, same luck

Every road runs the same hundred futures. In a given future the same free AI app goes viral and the same chain collapses whatever the board decided, so the differences come from the decisions.

Back to the board

A briefing shows what each decision did to students, teachers, fees and results, what was luck, and where the 2026 batch ended up. Then rewind and try another road.

The four roads

Two decisions, four roads, one hundred futures each

Each road shows where most of its hundred futures end, and what the board's two decisions did by 2036. That part is identical in every future; luck and ordinary ups and downs make up the rest.
